About Protoplanetary Disks

Planets form through chaotic collisions and gravitational accretion of planetesimals orbiting a newborn protostar.

  • Disk Log: Every 0.5 years: bodies beyond 5 AU, active bodies, and merges.
  • Merge Log: Each merge records both victims' masses, the result, and their distances from the star in the frame before the crash and the merged body's position in the frame after.
  • Planet-Moon Log: 2 times a year: small bound bodies that stay inside a bigger body's Hill sphere are alerted, with a count of consecutive years they have persisted.

Interaction: Click any body to inspect it. Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom.
